About ComodaDesk

At ComodaDesk, we blend architecture with personal style to create spaces that truly reflect who you are. We offer comprehensive design services for both interior and exterior environments, including detailed planning and project conceptualization. Our platform serves as a hub to showcase preliminary plans and visual inspiration for your projects. We also offer a curated product selection — sofas, lighting, and branded white goods like candles — to complement each unique design.

Our Design Process

1. Discovery & Consultation

We begin by understanding your space, needs, lifestyle and preferences through a one-on-one consultation. This step helps us capture your vision and personal style.

2. Planning & Inspiration

We create layout drafts, mood boards, and initial sketches — helping you visualize the direction of the project. This phase includes early architectural ideas and concepts.

3. Final Design & Product Selection

We finalize the design, provide a curated selection of products (sofas, lighting, candles and more), and prepare everything for implementation — all aligned with your preferences.
